PeakCut Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

PeakCut (also registered with TikTok as “shydo”) is a personal web tool that cuts long videos into short clips and publishes them to the owner’s own TikTok account. This policy explains what data the app uses.

What we access

When you click “Connect TikTok”, you log in through TikTok. With your permission, PeakCut receives your basic profile information (display name and avatar) through the user.info.basic scope, and permission to publish videos through the video.publish scope.

How we use it

Profile information is shown only to confirm which account is connected. Publishing permission is used only to post clips that you select, when you click the post button. Nothing is posted automatically.

Storage

Your videos and TikTok access tokens are stored only on the computer where PeakCut runs. They are not uploaded to any other server, except to TikTok when you publish a clip.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with third parties.

Retention and removal

You can disconnect TikTok inside PeakCut, delete the stored tokens and clips at any time, and revoke access from your TikTok account under Settings, Security and permissions, Apps and services.

Children

PeakCut is not intended for people under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the app owner, Chihab, through the contact details on the TikTok for Developers app listing.
