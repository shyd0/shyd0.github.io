PeakCut

Cut long videos into short clips and post them to TikTok.

What it does

PeakCut is a web tool that turns long videos into short, ready-to-post clips. After you connect your own TikTok account, you can publish a clip directly from PeakCut.

How it works with TikTok

  • Connect: you log in with TikTok (Login Kit). PeakCut reads only your basic profile info, such as display name and avatar, to show which account is connected.
  • Post: you pick a clip, write the caption and choose the privacy setting. PeakCut then publishes it to your account using the Content Posting API (Direct Post). Nothing is posted without your action.

Your data

Your videos and access tokens stay on the computer where PeakCut runs. Read the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service for details.
